# Source verification and limits

Primary McKinsey 2025 and 2026 pages, Stanford Economy chapter and DHL March briefing were inspected on 2026-11-17. The ledger records dates, observation periods, populations, denominator units and gaps. Source extracts are deliberately limited; the original URLs remain authoritative.

The 2026 subgroup sample counts were not verified and must not be inferred from total n. Stanford is a compilation drawing on McKinsey, not independent replication. Its exact publication day was not verified on the inspected primary page, so only 2026 is recorded. DHL is one operator example, not a survey; there is no adoption denominator. The old combined Trend Radar/briefing citation is replaced by the precisely dated March briefing for the booking claim. No new assertion about customs-payment autonomy is supported.

No source supplies the required comparable mid-sized 3PL payment-authority diffusion denominator, internal cost baseline, or exact consulting/municipal peer penetration. Those remain abstention conditions, not fabricated measurements. Recheck originals before consequential reuse.
